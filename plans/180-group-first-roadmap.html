Group-first Roadmap

From the corrected map to a Treehouse two-week pilot: the three loss gates built and tested, Toolshed folded in as a module, thread rollover as the volume policy, and a member-operated relay kept as a privacy option. What only the operator can supply

Decisions

  • D4 lease every founder-issued grant at genesis, with admin renewal (before 4.1). Recommended: yes.
  • D5 the isolation countersign making Toolshed a module (before 5.2 picks its package shape, and before 7.6).
  • D6 the AF-3 vouch shape: threshold, who may sign, what tombstoning an identity means (before 3.2).
  • D7 if D4 is declined, whether the first pilot accepts that an unleased founder-granted member cannot be removed after founder loss.

Hosting, secrets, devices, people

  • One supported Linux host, a DNS name, TCP 443, certificate renewal egress, an encrypted off-host backup destination, a separately held recovery key, a clean restore host, an owner for the 24-hour RPO (A2c).
  • Service identity, a root-bound catalog signer per product, TLS material and peer manifests through secret files with a custodian (A2b, A2c).
  • Device B, an unrelated physical Android, before any two-phone verdict (6.1); Device C for the Toolshed audit (7.6). iOS stays parked.
  • A 9 to 15 person community for a text-only pilot (6.2); 5 to 8 households for Toolshed (7.6).

Outside this roadmap

  • Federation, cross-space identity, universal tally (M6).
  • Key rotation, recovery, E2EE (M3).
  • Production compaction; coercion-resistant elections (the Stub stays frozen and false).
  • Host migration, any availability claim, any score or aggregate, any new op kind.
  • Township B waves, iOS, and the Township instrument reports (154, 156, 157) ahead of E1.

Stop conditions carried forward

  • Any founder-loss safety claim before AF-2 and AF-3 both pass; any member-loss claim before 3.2; any prohibited hosting phrase at any time.
  • Any hosting sentence that omits who can read or who can withhold.
  • Any numeric or ranked reputation; any push of negative reputation; any cross-community registry.
  • Any isolation change without the countersign; any BEAM-only or TypeScript-only semantic merge.
  • Any private key, seed or capability secret in a doc, fixture, log or test output.
